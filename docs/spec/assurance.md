### R87 — An assurance obligation records scope, question

Type: B,H · Scenarios: [A155](#a155), [A156](#a156), [A225](#a225)

An assurance obligation records scope, question, candidate, existing evidence and insufficiency. An overlapping second obligation on the same candidate names an uncovered risk, including when the first is withdrawn but its accepted proof remains current. A report is consumed only after exact native settlement and is immutable per Dispatch; exact replay is idempotent, while changed report or result evidence needs a fresh attempt. Findings record reviewer severity and triage: `required_correction` creates a parented correction obligation, consolidated on the candidate lineage; `advisory` creates a proposal; a downgraded blocker or major finding needs a reason. Several related findings of one attempt may share one new correction, retaining up to eight ids and highest severity. A related group carries up to eight `findings` members with individual finding/severity; advisory groups declare a common `root_cause`, while required groups name their shared correction. The stored entry preserves ids, highest severity and shared summary, counts once, replays idempotently and cannot regroup stored ids. Each assurance retains at most 64 finding records across distinct reports; one review attempt contributes at most 32, including incremental submissions. Exact replay spends no new slots; neither a request nor an accumulated attempt can supply 33 records. Bounds refuse atomically without truncation or eviction. A 65th retained record names the capacity blocker and the supported next action under R150, using accepted state facts rather than unaccepted correction dependencies; R151 permits a fresh identical row once its serving-result requirements are met, while no write frees a slot in the old history by resolving, withdrawing, regrouping or moving earlier findings. Existing finding content, attempt/source provenance, correction links, resolution order and withdrawal reasons retain their meaning. Earlier readers with the 32-record contract refuse larger finding histories during semantic validation without rewriting them. The obligation is satisfied by a completed, validated review observation bound to the current candidate, or a valid REUSE binding, together with satisfied or user-withdrawn corrections. A still-valid accepted proof relationship cannot be erased or restated to admit repeat review. Recording a required correction from any legitimately admitted completed review invalidates earlier accepted assurance proof, even when the report arrives late or old bindings return. Admission timestamps do not determine that order. Advisory additions cannot evict the correction's safety record; qualification waits for corrections and suitable current review proof. Definition-bound receipt history survives omission and candidate metadata changes. A correction affecting reviewed scope, assumptions, dependencies or governance, or with unknown impact, reopens the obligation for affected-scope delta review. Explicit candidate REUSE binding with the Git delta preserves unaffected evidence; stricter project policy governs. [R173](#r173--keep-findings-goal-scoped-and-bound-review-rounds) defines goal links and the review allowance.

### R150 — Retain 64 receipts separately from 16 selected evidence items

Type: I,H · Scenarios: [A501](#a501), [A502](#a502), [A503](#a503), [A530](#a530)

Each obligation retains at most 64 immutable receipts (`MAX_RECEIPTS`), while a write selects at most 16 evidence items (`MAX_EVIDENCE`). Contract discovery and diagnostics expose these independently. Criterion proof-scope history in `pod_ticks` follows the 64-receipt bound. A replay of an existing receipt spends no slot. A checkpoint or report that would retain a 65th receipt refuses `receipt_limit`, naming retained, proposed and limit counts, plus the pending admission to consume or, when none is pending, an exact next-sequence R151 `renew` request with a fresh-id placeholder. An assurance admission reserves one receipt and 32 finding records per outstanding unconsumed serving attempt, including itself: retained receipts plus those attempts must fit 64, and retained findings plus 32 times those attempts must fit 64. Otherwise admission refuses before launch with `receipt_limit` or `finding_limit`. If a serving report is pending, the refusal names its admission and directs consumption before retrying; a route-held attempt names the same admission and the project lead's applicable R161 settlement step. It offers the exact renewal request only when nothing is pending. Native settlement keeps the reservation until report consumption, except for R160's validated terminal route discard of an unconsumed review/investigation; deferred or ordinarily dispositioned implementation attempts reserve nothing. An unsettled route mismatch, an unknown effective route, a recoverable error or a native failure without a consumed report otherwise keeps the reservation and renewal hold, because Pod still accepts a later matching report. Terminal route discard preserves known required findings as ordinary corrections, releases reservations and the renewal hold atomically, and prohibits every later report as `route_settled_discarded`; neither lead acceptance alone nor a capacity-only discard supplies that exception. When no other serving report is pending, including when the refused write itself consumes the only one, the refusal gives the exact renewal request. Checkpoint receipts and incremental findings from older consumed reports cannot take another pending attempt's reserved slots; replay spends none. The renewal request uses accepted state facts, never dependencies introduced only by the refused write. It publishes neither context nor chunks. Nothing evicts, rewrites or borrows a receipt; the 512 KiB physical context bound and immutable chunk protections remain.

### R173 — Keep findings goal-scoped and bound review rounds

An R110 successor objective starts its own count. Three rounds are granted initially. Once those rounds are used, review admission refuses `review_allowance_reached` before native effects. A latest round without consumed settled report is running and asks nothing; a clean third round finishes normally. The coordinator asks through native mail, never a maintainer card or owner-question notice. Read-only report/status expose the derived state, rounds, granted count and question id.

The checkpoint boundary carries an unchanged recorded origin; caller-created or edited claims are cleared to unknown and their questions remain ordinary. Reads validate that source, admission ordinal, sequence, grant history and exact question/answer shape. An earlier unmarked row may be adopted only while its exact current due state is independently justified; unproven rows are never adopted merely by their labels or ids.

Owned questions and their sources survive carried, omitted, empty and public-round-tripped question inputs, clear assurance, answers, simplification, stop, renewal and later rounds. Existing accepted decision history supplies answered facts; no unanswered row is marked answered merely to hide a card. Public status keeps an owned unanswered question visible as `review_allowance_question`, excluding it from the owner feed even when it is no longer due. Answered history remains in the ordinary projection. Private origin metadata is not exposed. Ordinary, earlier, tool, sensitive, policy and lookalike rows remain visible; raw decision waits, needs_you, original owner-feed indices and bounds are preserved. This uses the existing question/history records, with no additional store, classifier, public API or host feature.

Only the coordinator's checkpoint `revision_authority` answering that recorded question grants an allowance. A lead answer uses R166's `agent_decision`/`agent_session` or verified decision path and carries `delegation: {basis: lead_decision, source: {reference}, reason}`, reusing the delegated route-settlement source shape. The reference identifies the lead's message; the reason explains this one answer. The coordinator relays the lead, whom Pod does not authenticate, and never labels that decision `user_direct`. Current machine decision policy still applies. Unsourced agent answers, reviewer/worker decisions and responder answers refuse; workers cannot checkpoint for the coordinator. An actual person's own session decision or generic host-verified person decision is accepted without delegation, though Pod never asks for it. Delegated source/reason stay in private revision history; public decision projections retain their digest, while answered question rows expose only the bounded answer.

The bounded `allowance` has `choice: stop|simplify|continue`, with integer `rounds: 1..8` required only for continue. When R rounds have been used at the answer, continue N grants through R+N; simplify must withdraw or revise at least one named obligation through the existing authorized procedure in the same write and grants through R+1, exactly one verification round. This also applies to objectives already beyond three rounds when the allowance is introduced. R171's correction-proof admission gate remains in force. Exhaustion asks again with the new rounds-used number if assurance remains unfinished. Stop prevents further review, and the existing interim report remains incomplete with every unfinished obligation listed. Silence grants nothing. Accepted answers retain kind `question`, channel, truthful provenance and delegation in revision decision history. Answered question rows retain top-level `resolved: true`, `status: answered` and `answer`. Repeated or changed answers cannot grant twice; earlier accepted owner-only answers remain readable. No new store, closure path or authority is introduced. Runtime guidance and review report-contract instructions belong to the installed verification reference under [R71](interface.md#r71--maintain-the-documentation-contract).
